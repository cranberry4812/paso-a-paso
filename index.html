Paso a Paso

English + early technology · Ages 4 to 7 · Online

Learn step by step, with Cami

Paso a Paso is a free online program for Spanish-speaking children. In 12 weeks, they learn English words, build early technology skills, and discover what architects, doctors, musicians and storytellers do.

Un programa gratuito en línea para niños hispanohablantes: inglés, tecnología y profesiones, paso a paso.

Become a partner Why it's good for families

Four missions, one guide

Cami goes on a new mission every three weeks. Each mission is designed with a volunteer professional and ends with a small showcase for families.
